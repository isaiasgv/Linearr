# Linearr — Business Requirements Document (BRD)

> The *business* problem, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, and
> constraints. Written before solution design starts. Audience: sponsors,
> leadership, product owner. See the PRD for the product's answer to this
> problem.

## Business problem

_What problem, opportunity, or pain point is driving this initiative? Who
feels this problem today, and what does it cost them (time, money, churn,
risk)?_

## Goals & objectives

_What does the business want to achieve? State goals so they're falsifiable
— "reduce support tickets" is vague; "reduce password-reset tickets by 30%
within Q3" is a goal._
