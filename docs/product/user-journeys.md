# Linearr — User Journeys

> The end-to-end, cross-session narrative of how a persona discovers,
> adopts, and gets value from the product over time. Broad and emotional —
> includes touchpoints outside the app (marketing email, support ticket,
> word of mouth). For the concrete in-app step sequence of a single task,
> see [user-flows.md](./user-flows.md) instead.
